Privacy policy

RoleSpace is a prototype run by the RoleSpace team (team leader: Praveen Saharan). This policy covers the public website at rolespace.duckdns.org and the demo workspace at rolespace.duckdns.org/rolespace-ent/. The demo runs on a made-up company, FirstRound, and its made-up people.

What we collect, and why

On the website

  • Work email in "Log in to your organization". We use it only to find your organization's workspace, then forget it. It isn't stored.
  • Your IP address, kept in memory for up to a minute so one visitor can't flood the lookup.
  • Standard request logs kept by Google Cloud: IP address, browser, page and time. We use them to keep the service running and secure.
  • The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

In the demo workspace

  • Google sign-in. Access goes through Google's Identity-Aware Proxy, which tells us your Google account's email address and sets a sign-in cookie. We use it only to let you in.
  • What you do in the demo:
    • your questions and the answers, saved per browser so you can reopen them;
    • access requests;
    • an audit log of decisions and admin actions: which demo person, what, when;
    • usage records per demo key: model, tokens, cost and time.
  • AI processing. Your questions are sent to AI models on Google Cloud Vertex AI in our own Google Cloud project, to produce the answers.
  • Please don't enter personal or confidential information. The demo is about a made-up codebase.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. Two services act for us:

  • Google Cloud hosts the website and the workspace, and runs the AI models.
  • GitHub receives pull requests opened from the demo. They are public, because the demo repository is public.

How long we keep it

  • Saved chats are deleted automatically 30 days after their last use.
  • Audit and usage records are kept while the prototype runs, and deleted when it shuts down.
  • Request logs follow Google Cloud's standard retention.

Your choices

You can delete your saved chats from History in the workspace. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, open an issue on GitHub. Don't put personal details in the issue; we'll reply there.

Children

RoleSpace is for professional use and isn't meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
