The role ladder: what each level can do

Each person gets one level in role.yaml. The level decides which tools their agent has (enforced in code), which models and budget their gateway key allows (enforced by the gateway), and whether they get a Coder workspace. Designer edits are limited to public/css/** and public/**/*.html, checked again in CI; every merge needs one human review.
