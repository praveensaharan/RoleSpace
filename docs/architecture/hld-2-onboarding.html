From a line in role.yaml to a ready workspace

Today an admin gives a person a level in role.yaml. provision_keys.py creates their gateway key with the level's models and budget; for Build, sync_build_keys.sh puts the key on the Coder VM, and only people with a key there can create a workspace. On day one the developer opens Coder through the IAP tunnel, with no VPN; the workspace is ready in about 30 seconds with Gemini CLI on their own key, and their first call shows in Govern. In production, groups would sync from Entra ID (dashed: designed, not built).
